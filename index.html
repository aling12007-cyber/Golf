HOKKAIDO

Hokkaido

Niseko Village Golf Course

Perched at the base of Niseko’s Hanazono ski area with sweeping views of Mt Yotei, Niseko Village Golf Course delivers a scenic, resort-style round through silver birch forests and rolling fairways—ideal for high-end travellers seeking iconic Hokkaido landscapes and polished service in a world-class mountain destination.

The 18-hole, par-73 Prince Course stretches to 6,845 yards with bentgrass greens, strategic water hazards and minimal out-of-bounds, offering a fair yet engaging challenge for all levels. Full rental sets, GPS-equipped carts, a well-stocked pro shop, and seamless access from Hilton Niseko Village and Hirafu make it a convenient, premium addition to any Niseko golf itinerary.

Hokkaido

Furano Golf Course

Set amid the rolling hills and forests of central Hokkaido, Furano Golf Course delivers an Arnold Palmer–designed, 36-hole resort experience that blends championship challenge with breathtaking Daisetsuzan mountain vistas.

The Palmer Course offers a long, strategic test while the King Course provides a more accessible round, both featuring wide fairways, natural elevation changes, GPS-equipped ride-on carts, rental sets, a pro shop, driving range and on-site restaurant.

Google Maps
Hokkaido

Gozensui Golf Club

Just 15 minutes from an international airport, Gozensui Golf Club offers a serene, forested 18-hole layout that blends traditional Japanese course aesthetics with immaculate conditioning.

Carved through mature hardwoods with narrow, tree-lined fairways, strategic white-sand bunkers and subtle water features, the par-72 course plays to 7,013 yards from the tips and demands accuracy over power.

Google Maps
Hokkaido

A-Brand Golf Club

One of Hokkaido’s premier golf clubs, A-Brand is an 18-hole jewel with undulating greens, significant elevation changes, tactical bunkers and impressive sea views. A major draw is the opulent clubhouse inspired by the fairy tale castles of northern Europe.

— Tohoku

MIYAGI

Miyagi

Izumi Kokusai Golf Course

Sendai city’s premier course balances tradition and technology in a serene, natural setting. Nestled in the forested foothills of Izumigatake, it delivers a refined championship-calibre round with classic Japanese course design and modern conveniences.

Its reputation for excellent conditioning, strategic yet fair design and comprehensive facilities makes it a compelling destination for golf-loving visitors to northern Japan.

— Greater Tokyo

SAITAMA

Saitama

Kiyosumi Golf Club

Just 50 km northwest of central Tokyo, Kiyosumi delivers a championship-calibre, forested 18-hole experience designed by Shō Ōkubo.

The par-72 layout stretches to 7,084 yards across gently rolling, tree-lined terrain, with wide strategic fairways, undulating bentgrass greens and subtle water features.

Google Maps
— Pacific Coast

SHIZUOKA

Shizuoka

Kawana Hotel Golf Course

Perched on the dramatic Pacific coastline of the Izu Peninsula, the Fuji Course at Kawana Hotel is widely regarded as one of Japan’s finest public-access resort layouts.

Designed by Charles Hugh Alison and opened in 1936, the course weaves through coastal cliffs, pine forests and undulating fairways with sweeping Suruga Bay views.

Google Maps
Shizuoka

Daifuji Golf Course

Set at the base of Mount Fuji, Daifuji Golf Club offers a compact, strategic 18-hole test that blends classic Japanese course character with links-inspired undulations.

At 6,448 yards and par 72, the course rewards accuracy over distance with rolling fairways, subtle elevation changes and fast, true greens.

Google Maps
— Mount Fuji

YAMANASHI

Yamanashi

Fuji Classic

A world-class 18-hole championship golf course at an elevation of 1,200 metres at the foot of Mount Fuji, created by renowned architect Desmond Muirhead and inspired by Hokusai’s Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji.

Google Maps
Yamanashi

Narusawa Golf Club

Set on the northern slopes of Mount Fuji at 1,100 metres elevation, Narusawa Golf Club delivers a prestigious walking-only 18-hole experience renowned for immaculate conditioning, fast bentgrass greens and iconic Fuji views.

— Kansai

KYOTO

Kyoto

Seta Golf Course

Just 20 minutes from Kyoto and perched on the hills overlooking Lake Biwa and Mount Hiei, Seta Golf Course is a prestigious 54-hole complex designed by Seiichi Inoue.

The North Course is a championship benchmark in Kansai, combining expansive tree-lined fairways, subtle elevation changes, water features and fast bentgrass greens.

Set in mature hardwood forest just north of central Osaka, Ibaraki Country Club is a storied 36-hole private facility renowned for its championship pedigree and architectural lineage.

OITA

Oita

Beppu Golf Club

Founded in 1930 and set high above Beppu Bay, Beppu Golf Club is one of Kyushu’s most historic golfing venues. The 36-hole complex combines mountain and sea views with classic Japanese parkland character.

Miyazaki

Phoenix Country Club

Set within a majestic black-pine forest along Miyazaki’s Pacific coast, Phoenix Country Club is one of Japan’s most prestigious tournament venues. Its 27 holes combine strategic shot-making with a distinctive coastal atmosphere.
