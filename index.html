HOKKAIDO

Hokkaido

Niseko Village Golf Course

Perched at the base of Niseko’s Hanazono ski area with sweeping views of Mt Yotei, Niseko Village Golf Course delivers a scenic, resort-style round through silver birch forests and rolling fairways—ideal for high-end travellers seeking iconic Hokkaido landscapes and polished service in a world-class mountain destination.

The 18-hole, par-73 Prince Course stretches to 6,845 yards with bentgrass greens, strategic water hazards and minimal out-of-bounds, offering a fair yet engaging challenge for all levels. Full rental sets, GPS-equipped carts, a well-stocked pro shop, and seamless access from Hilton Niseko Village and Hirafu (under 10 minutes) make it a convenient, premium addition to any Niseko golf itinerary.

Hokkaido

Furano Golf Course

Set amid the rolling hills and forests of central Hokkaido, Furano Golf Course delivers an Arnold Palmer–designed, 36-hole resort experience that blends championship challenge with breathtaking Daisetsuzan mountain vistas—perfect for discerning inbound golfers seeking scenic, high-quality play in Japan’s premier summer golf destination.

The Palmer Course offers a long, strategic test suited to low-handicap players, while the King Course provides a more accessible yet engaging round for all abilities, both featuring wide fairways, natural elevation changes, and GPS-equipped ride-on carts allowed on fairways. Full rental sets, a pro shop, driving range and on-site restaurant serving local cuisine complete a polished, resort-style package ideal for Hokkaido golf itineraries.

Hokkaido

Gozensui Golf Club

Just 15 minutes from an international airport, Gozensui Golf Club offers a serene, forested 18-hole layout that blends traditional Japanese course aesthetics with immaculate conditioning. Perfect for discerning inbound golfers arriving on early flights or seeking a tranquil pre- or post-round near Sapporo.

Carved through mature hardwoods with narrow, tree-lined fairways, strategic white-sand bunkers and subtle water features, the par-72 course plays to 7,013 yards from the tips and demands accuracy over power. Fast, smooth greens, a minimalist yet elegant clubhouse, full locker-room amenities (including sauna and pool), and on-site dining round out a refined, high-service and memorable experience.

Hokkaido

A-Brand Golf Club

One of Hokkaido’s premier golf clubs, A-Brand is an 18-hole jewel. It boasts undulating emerald greens, significant elevation changes, tactical bunkers and impressive sea views thanks to the vision of golf course architect Kentaro Sato. A major draw is the opulent clubhouse, inspired by the fairy tale castles of northern Europe (the marble hall features taxidermied polar bears and wolves). The adjacent hotel serves delicious cuisine in its high-end restaurant, featuring fresh local ingredients and seasonal produce.

MIYAGI

Miyagi

Izumi Kokusai Golf Course

Sendai city’s premier course balances tradition and technology in a serene, natural setting. Izumi Kokusai offers a taste of authentic Japanese forest golf without compromising on comfort or convenience. Nestled in the forested foothills of Izumigatake, Izumi Kokusai Golf delivers a refined, championship-calibre round that combines classic Japanese course design with modern conveniences—ideal for those seeking authenticity, tranquillity and strategic play.

Its reputation for excellent course conditioning, strategic yet fair design and comprehensive facilities positions it as a compelling destination for golf loving visitors to northern Japan.

— Greater Tokyo

SAITAMA

Saitama

Kiyosumi Golf Club

Just 50 km northwest of central Tokyo, Kiyosumi delivers a championship-calibre, forested 18-hole experience designed by Shō Ōkubo. Ideal for discerning golfers seeking a traditional, full-service Japanese round with easy access from the capital.

The par-72 layout stretches to 7,084 yards across gently rolling, tree-lined terrain, blending wide, strategic fairways with undulating bentgrass greens and subtle water features to reward accuracy and course management. Caddie service is required, electric carts are provided and the elegant clubhouse offers a restaurant, pro shop, and on-site bath—creating a polished, high-touch experience suited to day-trip itineraries from Tokyo.

— Pacific Coast

SHIZUOKA

Shizuoka

Kawana Hotel Golf Course

Perched on the dramatic Pacific coastline of the Izu Peninsula, the Fuji Course at Kawana Hotel is widely regarded as Japan’s finest public-access resort layout and a bucket-list experience for discerning golfers seeking world-class design, ocean vistas and historic luxury just two hours from Tokyo.

Designed by Charles Hugh Alison and opened in 1936, the par-72, 6,701-yard course weaves through coastal cliffs, pine forests and undulating fairways, culminating in the signature par-5 15th with sweeping Suruga Bay views. Ranked among Golf Digest’s World’s 100 Greatest Courses, it hosts the LPGA’s Fuji-Sankei Ladies Classic and delivers immaculate conditioning, caddie service and seamless access to Kawana Hotel’s century-old hot-spring resort—making it a cornerstone of any premium golf itinerary.

Shizuoka

Daifuji Golf Course

Set at the base of Mount Fuji, Daifuji Golf Club offers a compact, strategic 18-hole test that blends classic Japanese course character with links-inspired undulations—ideal for those seeking an authentic, high-condition round with iconic mountain views.

At 6,448 yards and par 72, the course, built without modern landscaping machinery, rewards accuracy over distance, with rolling fairways, subtle elevation changes and fast, true greens demanding thoughtful shot placement. Opened in 1958, it maintains a traditional, members-club atmosphere with excellent conditioning, a welcoming clubhouse, and straightforward access positioning it as a compelling half-day or add-on round for Honshu golf itineraries.

YAMANASHI

Yamanashi

Fuji Classic Country Club

A world-class, 18-hole championship golf course located at an elevation of 1,200 meters at the foot of Mount Fuji in Yamanashi Prefecture, Japan. Created by renowned architect Desmond Muirhead. Inspired by Katsushika Hokusai’s famous woodblock print series Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji, with each hole reflecting a unique artistic concept. Famous for being the closest golf course to Mount Fuji, offering clear, majestic views of the peak from every single hole, alongside distant vistas of the Minami Alps.

Yamanashi

Narusawa Golf Club

Set on the northern slopes of Mount Fuji at 1,100 metres elevation, Narusawa Golf Club delivers a prestigious, walking-only 18-hole experience renowned for immaculate conditioning, fast bentgrass greens and volcanic-ash fairways that stay firm even after rain. Ideal for discerning golfers seeking an authentic, high-end Japanese club atmosphere with iconic Fuji views.

Designed by Mitsuaki Kobayashi and opened in 1992, the par-72, 7,017-yard layout features wide, tree-lined fairways, strategically placed ponds (notably the photogenic par-3 8th with Fuji reflected in the water) and a floating-island green on the 18th. Access is typically via member invitation only, with full rental sets, a traditional clubhouse, restaurant, and on-site bath completing a refined, members-club experience.

— Kansai

KYOTO

Kyoto

Seta Golf Course

Just 20 minutes from Kyoto and perched on the hills overlooking Lake Biwa and Mount Hiei, Seta Golf Course is a prestigious 54-hole complex designed by legendary architect Seiichi Inoue. Headlined by the North Course, host to the LPGA’s TOTO Japan Classic and a benchmark for championship-calibre golf in the Kansai region.

The par-72 North Course stretches to 7,012 yards from the tips, blending expansive, tree-lined fairways with subtle elevation changes, strategically placed water features and fast, immaculate Mackenzie bentgrass greens that demand precision and thoughtful course management. Every round includes caddie service and GPS-equipped riding carts, with a dedicated jacuzzi bath for North Course players, a 15-bay driving range, pro shop and restaurant completing a high-service experience. Seamless integration with Lake Biwa Otsu Prince Hotel make Seta an ideal premium day-trip or overnight stop for Kansai golf itineraries.

OSAKA

Osaka

Ibaraki Country Club

Set in mature hardwood forest just 30 minutes north of central Osaka, Ibaraki Country Club is a storied 36-hole private facility renowned for its championship pedigree and architectural lineage. Featuring the East Course, remodelled in 1930 by Charles Hugh Alison and the West Course, completely redesigned in 2011 by Rees Jones (his first Japanese project) and widely regarded as one of Asia’s finest inland tests.

The West Course stretches to 7,407 yards from the tips, blending tree-lined parkland fairways with deep strategic bunkering, subtle elevation changes and fast, immaculate bentgrass greens that reward precision and thoughtful course management. Access traditionally requires a member introduction. The East Course offers a more accessible yet equally refined alternative, with caddie service, motorised carts, a well-appointed clubhouse and practice range positioning Ibaraki CC as a compelling stop for Kansai golf itineraries targeting Osaka’s most prestigious inland layouts.

OITA

Oita

Beppu Golf Club

Set in the historic Kitsuki highlands of Oita, Beppu Golf Club delivers a classic, members-club atmosphere across two 18-hole courses that blend traditional Japanese design with undulating, forested terrain and panoramic views of Beppu Bay. Perfect for golfers seeking authentic, high-condition golf in Japan’s premier hot-spring region.

The historic 36-hole facility features wide, strategic fairways, subtle elevation changes and well-guarded greens that reward accuracy and course management. Full rental sets, caddie service, a traditional clubhouse with restaurant and on-site bath complete a refined, high-service experience suited to luxury Kyushu golf programmes.

MIYAZAKI

Miyazaki

Phoenix Country Club

Situated on the coastal plains of Miyazaki, Phoenix Country Club is known for its tournament pedigree, immaculate conditioning and striking white-sand bunkers framed by tall black pines. A compelling stop for discerning golfers seeking a championship-calibre Kyushu experience.

The 27-hole complex lies along the Hitotsuba Pacific Coast and has hosted the Dunlop Phoenix Tournament since 1974. Its seaside winds, pine-lined fairways and full-service resort amenities position it as a premium, tournament-grade option for Kyushu golf itineraries.

KAGOSHIMA

Kagoshima

Ibusuki Golf Course

Set on the southern tip of the Satsuma Peninsula with sweeping views of Kagoshima Bay, the Pacific Ocean and the iconic Mount Kaimon, Ibusuki Golf Club is a championship-calibre masterpiece designed by Seiichi Inoue and a bucket-list stop for discerning golfers seeking world-class design in a dramatic seaside setting.

The par-72 layout weaves across undulating coastal hills, blending wide, tree-framed fairways with deep strategic bunkering and the ever-present challenge of crosswinds from the mountain and sea. Full caddie service, a well-appointed clubhouse and seamless integration with Iwasaki Resort Ibusuki create a polished, high-end resort experience.
