Hokkaido · Niseko

Niseko Village Golf Course

A grand prize winner at the World Golf Awards, Niseko Village Golf Course is set amongst pretty silver birch forests, with stunning Mount Yotei serving as a cinematic backdrop. Across 18 challenging holes, you’ll experience long manicured fairways peppered with a range of strategic hazards.

Hokkaido

Furano Golf Course

Classic open, lush Hokkaido landscape with two 18-hole layouts including one designed by Arnold Palmer. On rolling hills surrounded by mountain vistas similar to Whistler valley.

Hokkaido

Gozensui Golf Club

A scenic, well-presented, championship-style option; conveniently near Shinchitose international airport. Clear streams, ponds and preserved natural landscape create a tranquil atmosphere that can best be described as Hokkaido’s Augusta. Excellent clubhouse restaurant serves local cuisine.

Hokkaido

A-Brand Golf Club

One of Hokkaido’s premier golf clubs, A-Brand is an 18-hole jewel. It boasts undulating emerald greens, significant elevation changes, tactical bunkers and impressive sea views thanks to architect Kentaro Sato. The opulent clubhouse is inspired by the fairy tale castles of northern Europe, while the adjacent hotel serves cuisine featuring fresh local ingredients and seasonal produce.

Shizuoka

Mt. Fuji & the coast
Shizuoka

Kawana Hotel Golf Course

Opened in 1936 and designed by renowned golf architect C. H. Alison, often called the “Pebble Beach of Japan”. Known for steep hills, deep, menacing bunkers, small putting surfaces and spectacular coastal views. It is accessible from Tokyo and regularly ranks among the top 100 golf courses globally.

Shizuoka

Daifuji Golf Course

A historic, traditional 18-hole golf course located in Shizuoka Prefecture. Opened in 1954, during an era lacking heavy machinery, it was crafted largely by hand using picks and shovels, preserving the natural terrain. Designed by agricultural doctor Nobukatsu Maruge, the layout mirrors a traditional Scottish links course and offers panoramic views of Mt Fuji and Suruga Bay.

Yamanashi

At the foot of Mount Fuji
Yamanashi

Fuji Classic Country Club

A world-class, 18-hole championship golf course located at an elevation of 1,200 meters at the foot of Mount Fuji. Created by renowned architect Desmond Muirhead and inspired by Katsushika Hokusai’s Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji, with each hole reflecting a unique artistic concept. Famous for offering clear, majestic views of the peak from every single hole.

Yamanashi

Narusawa Golf Club

Opened in 1993, this woodland course features wide, largely flat fairways separated by groves of Japanese red pine and white birch. Sitting 1,100 metres above sea level, it provides cool, refreshing playing conditions during the summer months, with Mt. Fuji views and a calm, traditionally Japanese atmosphere.

Kyushu

Oita · Miyazaki · Kagoshima
Oita

Beppu Golf Club

One of Kyushu’s oldest clubs (36 holes); the Yufu Course offers dramatic downhill holes and mountain vistas. Tsurumi course has sea views over the Bungo Channel. A classic, historic club atmosphere.

Miyazaki

Phoenix Country Club

Resort-style championship venue; rated top 100 in the world and top 3 in Japan; visitor-friendly but books out especially on weekends; Japan tour pedigree. Direct flights from Seoul.

Kagoshima

Ibusuki Golf Course

Prestigious course designed by Seichi Inoue; on rolling hills at foot of Mt Kaimon, a near perfect volcanic cone rising from the sea. Views of both the mountain and East China Sea give a uniquely Japanese coastal-mountain drama.
